The result was verified, not assumed, to genuinely improve performance at every level checked — training-adjacent validation, held-out direct-classifier evaluation, and held-out full-pipeline decision quality — and was promoted to production. The manuscript was updated to match, and a full consistency/reproducibility audit confirms no stale numbers remain for the primary (v2.5b) results.
